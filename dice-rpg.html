Dice Quest

How to play

Move with the arrow keys / WASD or the pad (the pad hides during turn-based fights). Regions read stage-region, so 2-8-b is stage 2, region 8, side path b. There are 10 stages; the last holds Lucifugus, the Final Boss.

Classes & weapons: pick a class at the start; change it any time in the 🎓 Class Hall (half your credits). Each weapon type (Sword, Gauntlets, Dagger, Bow, Staff) only powers its own skills; “Any weapon” skills always work.

Fights: attacker and defender each roll a d20. If the attacker wins, roll a d6 for damage: 1 = 0% (25% with a Fighter’s Band), 2 = 25%, 3 = 50%, 4 = 75%, 5 = 100%, 6 = 125%. Skills of 100%+ damage also duel on the d20 with the bonus shown.

SP: skills cost SP. You regain SP every turn and every 5 steps (faster at 20, 40, 60+ max SP), 50% after a win, and fully in Safeplaces and shops.

Boss arenas (every 10th region) start the moment you enter and seal the gates. Attack from within your weapon’s range (diagonals count as 2 tiles); ranged skills show their reach (↗). The boss acts every 3 of your steps (2 below half HP) and its counterattack uses up its turn. Red tiles are hit on its next move(s); black tiles mean instant death (8th boss and beyond).

Achievements unlock skins, which you can wear at the 🧵 Tailor in any Safeplace. Stage 3+ bosses also leave lingering ground: red = hit next move, then purple = it keeps hurting. Secrets await those who beat the final boss with every class...

Safeplaces (1st region of each stage) have a 🏪 shop, 🏫 skill hall, 🏛️ guild and 🎓 class hall. 🌀 / 🔒 gates lead to side paths (b to e) ending in a block puzzle, a riddle chamber, a mini boss arena, or an exclusive shop.
